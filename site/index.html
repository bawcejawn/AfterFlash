Questions

Do guests need an app or an account?

No. They scan the QR code and the camera opens in their phone’s browser. No downloads, no sign-ups.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once for one event, 1 month or 12 months. Nothing renews automatically. Storage is your own Google Drive, so there’s nothing else to pay us.

What’s the difference between the plans?

1 event: one event, open for 30 days from purchase. 1 month and 12 months: as many events as you like during that time. Buying again while signed in adds time (or another event) to the same admin key.

What happens when my plan ends?

You can’t create events, open dashboards or take new photos until you renew; guests’ phones keep any photos and upload them after you renew. Your photos stay in your Google Drive forever.

How long do albums stay online?

Album and guest links stay online while your plan is active and for 12 months after it ends. After that the links close, but every photo is still in your Google Drive. Renewing reopens them. Download or share from Drive before then if guests will want them later.

Can I get a refund?

All sales are final. If something doesn’t work because of a problem on our side, email us: we’ll fix it, and where it’s fair we’ll add time to your key.

I have a referral code.

Enter it under the plans before you pay, or open the link you were sent. The discount shows on the price before checkout.

Can I use it for clients?

Yes. Planners, venues and photographers use one key for all their clients’ events and charge whatever they like. Each event gets its own host link to hand to the couple.

What can Rememberoll see in my Google Drive?

Only the files it creates for you. Google enforces this: Rememberoll can’t open anything else in your Drive.

I don’t have a Gmail account.

Making one is free and takes two minutes. The admin page walks you through it.

What if I lose my admin key?

On the sign-in page, choose “Sign in with Google” with the Google account you connected, and you’ll get a new key.
