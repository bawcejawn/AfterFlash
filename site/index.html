How it works

  1. Create the event

    Name it, set how many shots each guest gets, and choose whether photos appear live or develop after the party.

  2. Put the QR code out

    Print the sign or show it on the TV wall. Guests scan it and the camera opens in their browser. Nothing to install.

  3. Collect the album

    Moderate as it happens, then download every original from your own Google Drive.

Built for the day itself

Works on bad signal

Photos save on the guest’s phone first and upload when there’s a connection. Nothing gets lost to venue Wi-Fi.

A real roll of film

A limited shot count, film looks baked in at capture, and an optional date stamp. Guests shoot more carefully, and the album is better for it.

Live photo wall

Open the TV link on any screen. New photos appear in seconds, with the QR code beside them so more guests join.

You stay in control

Approve photos before they show, hide or delete anything, pause uploads, and block a guest if needed. Guests can flag a photo, and it disappears until you decide.

Private by default

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Location data is stripped from every photo. Files live in your Drive, not someone else’s.

Permanent keepsakes

Optionally archive the album on the Koinos blockchain, with a fingerprint that proves every original is untouched.
