# Rememberoll roadmap: 50 more ideas

The rule for everything here: **a guest never needs instructions.** Every idea is either one tap for guests, or a switch the host turns on. If an idea needs a menu to explain it, it waits.

There are **no ads, ever.** Money comes from the plan and from optional add-ons that someone chooses to buy. Each money idea below says who pays.

Status: **Done** = live today. **Next** = small and worth doing soon. **Later** = good, but needs more work or real customer demand first.

## Already built (for reference)

Camera with no app download, disposable-camera mode (limited shots, develop later), film looks, shared album, TV slideshow, guestbook with voice messages, highlight video, invitations with RSVP, photo challenges, captions, phone app home screen, automatic messages and thank-you notes, prints and photo books, planner branding and event copies.

## Fun for guests (the "toy" part)

1. **Timed challenges.** At a set time the camera tells everyone "Photo with someone you just met!"; the TV shows the results. *Next.*
2. **Stickers.** A few stamps (names, date, a heart) guests can add to a photo. *Later.*
3. **Photo bingo card.** The challenge list shown as a bingo grid; finishing a line throws confetti. *Next.*
4. **Reactions.** Heart, laugh or fire on any album photo; no comments, so nothing to moderate. *Next.*
5. **"Best of the night" vote.** Guests get 3 votes; the top 10 show on the TV at the end. *Later.*
6. **Live photo wall mode.** New photos fly onto the TV as they arrive. *Next.*
7. **Countdown screen.** The invitation shows "12 days to go" and changes into the camera on the day. *Next.*
8. **"My photos" page.** Each guest can see and download just the photos they took. *Next.*
9. **Video guestbook prompts.** "Give the couple one piece of advice" before recording. *Next.*
10. **Table challenges.** Each table gets its own challenge and the TV shows a table leaderboard. *Later.*
11. **Photo booth frame.** The host uploads a frame or picks one, and guests can add it to a photo. *Later.*
12. **"Find yourself."** A guest takes a selfie and the album shows the photos they're in, matched on their own phone only. *Later; privacy review first.*
13. **Scavenger hunt.** Hidden QR codes around the venue unlock bonus challenges. *Later.*
14. **Time capsule.** Guests leave a message that unlocks on the first anniversary. *Later; it's also a paid add-on (see 33).*
15. **Song request wall.** The songs from RSVPs show on a screen for the DJ. *Next.*

## Easy for hosts

16. **One-page setup.** Event name, date, done; everything else stays optional. *Next.*
17. **Templates.** Wedding, birthday, baby shower, corporate, reunion: each fills in challenges, messages and look. *Next.*
18. **Co-host link.** Share hosting with a partner without sharing a password. *Next.*
19. **Hide a photo in one tap,** straight from the TV or album. *Next.*
20. **Album download as one zip,** sorted into folders by challenge. *Next.*
21. **Printable table cards.** A QR code plus "Scan to add your photos" in the event's colours, as a PDF. *Next.*
22. **Seating chart from RSVPs.** Drag names onto tables. *Later.*
23. **Gift list notes.** Hosts note who gave what so thank-you notes are easier. *Later.*
24. **Day-of schedule.** "Dinner 7pm, first dance 8pm" shown on the invitation and TV. *Next.*
25. **Multi-day events.** Rehearsal, wedding and brunch under one invitation. *Later.*
26. **Guest photo approval.** Photos wait for the host before they go public. *Done ("need a look").*
27. **Recap email.** The day after: how many photos, the top 5, and a link. *Next (uses messages).*
28. **Text messages (SMS) for reminders.** Paid per use, because texts cost money. *Later.*

## Ways to make money (no ads)

29. **Prints.** Hosts order prints of chosen photos. You set the price and earn the margin. *Done.*
30. **Photo book.** A 20-page hardcover book from the best photos. High margin. *Done.*
31. **Rememberoll Storage.** We keep the originals for the host, so they don't need Google Drive. Costs us about $0.015 per GB a month (Cloudflare R2), so we sell it for $10–15 per event for 1 year. *Next.*
32. **Keep-it-forever.** A one-time upgrade that keeps the album and invitation online past the plan. *Later.*
33. **Time capsule unlock.** $5 to send the anniversary capsule (idea 14). *Later.*
34. **Highlight video in HD with music.** The free version stays; a paid version gets licensed music and longer cuts. *Later.*
35. **Premium invitation designs.** Free basic design; $5–10 for a premium design pack. *Next.*
36. **Custom link name.** rememberoll.app/SamAndAlex for $5. *Next.*
37. **Extra guests or videos** beyond the plan limits, as a one-time top-up. *Later.*
38. **Gift for the hosts.** A guest buys the photo book for the couple as their gift. *Later; big wedding market.*
39. **Planner tools.** "Planned by" branding and "Start from" event copies, included with the month and 12-month plans. *Done.* A separate higher-priced planner plan can come with white label (40).
40. **White label.** A planner's own logo and colours, with no Rememberoll branding. A higher planner tier. *Later.*
41. **Referral partners.** Partners earn a cut for every plan they sell. *Done.*
42. **Venue QR sign kit.** A printed acrylic sign mailed to the host. You mark up the print cost. *Later.*
43. **Corporate events.** A higher price, a branded TV wall and a lead-capture option for brands. *Later.*
